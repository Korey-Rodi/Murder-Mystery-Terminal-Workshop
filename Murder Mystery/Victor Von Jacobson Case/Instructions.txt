The goal of this workshop is to walk away knowing/have done the following..
- What github is and have created an account
- Understand the terminal and how to navigate it using commands
- Work with a new exciting Python Library
- Work with documentation


You can work alone or with a group to complete this workshop.
You or your team should be the first to complete the mystery before anyone else
You must collect all pieces of evidence; all cases must be complete for
a winner to be declared.

The goal is to have fun and learn be patient and dont take anything to seriously.
Hopefully you are introduced to some new and exciting concepts witin computing
and you can share or explore more in the future.

Have fun! and Happy Murder!
