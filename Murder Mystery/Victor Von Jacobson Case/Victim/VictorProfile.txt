Victor von Jacobson built a fortune not through honest enterprise, but through ruthless exploitation cloaked in aristocratic prestige. He made his millions as an asset-stripping venture capitalist, predatory lender, and blackmailer. He bought out failing family businesses only to gut their pension funds, foreclosed on vulnerable estates through hidden predatory clauses, and acquired personal debts specifically to hold ruinous leverage over high society.

Tonight, on Halloween, the music abruptly stopped when Victor was found dead out on the manor balcony.
